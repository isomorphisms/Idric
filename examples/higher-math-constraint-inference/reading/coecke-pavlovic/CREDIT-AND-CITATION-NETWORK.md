# Credit and citation network — Coecke / Pavlović

The purpose of this file is to keep the nearby intellectual network visible without confusing citation with coauthorship.

## Direct authors and close follow-on work

- **Bob Coecke** — coauthor of both primary works used here.
- **Duško Pavlović** — coauthor of both primary works used here.
- **Jamie Vicary** — coauthor of “A new description of orthogonal bases.”
- **Eric Oliver Paquette** — with Coecke, extended the sum-free graphical treatment to POVMs and Naimark's theorem.
- **Ross Duncan** — major collaborator in the development of interacting-observable / graphical quantum calculi.

## Categorical and graphical foundations cited by the papers

The papers explicitly situate themselves among work by, among others:

- **Samson Abramsky** — categorical semantics of quantum protocols with Coecke;
- **Peter Selinger** — dagger compact categories, completely positive maps, and graphical coherence;
- **Saunders Mac Lane** — monoidal/category-theoretic foundations;
- **G. M. Kelly** and **M. L. Laplaza** — coherence for compact closed categories;
- **André Joyal** and **Ross Street** — geometry of tensor calculus / graphical monoidal reasoning;
- **André Joyal**, **Ross Street**, and **Dominic Verity** — traced monoidal categories;
- **John Baez** — category-theoretic exposition of quantum structures;
- **Louis Kauffman** — related topological/diagrammatic approaches to teleportation;
- **W. K. Wootters** and **W. H. Zurek** — no-cloning;
- **Arun K. Pati** and **Samuel L. Braunstein** — no-deleting.

The papers also connect to the broader history of quantum measurement, bases, and Frobenius structures. When any particular theorem or equation is copied into Idriç's mathematical corpus, use the paper's own bibliography to cite the exact source rather than attributing the whole lineage to Coecke/Pavlović.

## Why attribution matters to the compiler notes

A rewrite rule should carry mathematical provenance. If a future rule is “spider fusion,” “specialness,” “Frobenius,” “compact yanking,” or a no-cloning restriction, its explanation should name the relevant structure/law and source. The compiler's theorem index should not flatten distinct results into an anonymous category called “quantum diagram stuff.”
