## Graphical/group-theoretic lineage

The book and its public literature pages point backward through several graphical traditions. Particularly relevant to this reading thread are:

- **Roger Penrose** — graphical tensor notation;
- **I. B. Levinson** — early graphical representation of Wigner coefficients;
- **A. P. Yutsis, I. B. Levinson, V. V. Vanagas** — graphical angular-momentum calculus;
- **Eugene Wigner** — recoupling coefficients and representation-theoretic machinery;
- **Élie Cartan**, **Wilhelm Killing**, **H. S. M. Coxeter**, **Eugene Dynkin** — classification/canonical approaches to Lie theory against which the tensorial route is compared.

The literature page at https://birdtracks.eu/refs/ is the maintained jumping-off point for the wider bibliography and later birdtrack work.

## Rule for reuse here

When a specific identity enters Idriç, add the direct source next to the rule. “Cvitanović says birdtracks” is not enough provenance for a rewrite whose actual origin is Penrose, Wigner/Yutsis, a named theorem, or another cited paper.
