## Source notes

The editors describe continuity as a family of concerns admitting rigorous but
incompatible sharpenings. Different theories retain different combinations
of historically important properties. Their position does not make those
theories interchangeable or exempt them from mathematical constraints.

## Connection to Idriç — our interpretation

Do not settle the semantics of continuous quantity merely by selecting a
machine numeric type. Ask what the quantity means before choosing its
coordinates and computational representation.

This collection supplies a research route, not a mandate to replace all
existing mathematics in the compiler. Keep separate theories and their
translations explicit. The language should make assumptions available to a
human or LLM reader instead of quietly importing them from a familiar model.
